About the Project

The project aims to ground suspicion in legally assessable reasons, control error rates while maintaining fairness across intersecting protected groups, and provide explanations that support both legal and meaningful contestation. Because a single statute covers fraud across social insurance, study aid, unemployment, and housing support, the method extends to all four domains.
